# Batches and timings

## JEE and NEET
Weekday evening batches run from 5 pm to 8 pm, Monday to Friday. Weekend batches run on Saturday and Sunday from 9 am to 2 pm. Each batch has at most 40 students.

## Class 10 boards
Maths and Science batches run on weekdays from 4 pm to 6 pm, and there is a weekend batch on Saturday and Sunday mornings.

## Missed classes
Recorded lectures and notes for every class are uploaded to the student portal within 24 hours. Doubt-solving sessions run every Saturday afternoon.
