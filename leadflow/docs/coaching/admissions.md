# Admissions at BrightPath

## How to join
Attend a free demo class, take a short placement test, and then complete admission at the Dadar West centre with a passport-size photo and the student's latest marksheet.

## Instalments and scholarships
Fees can be paid in 3 instalments. Our scholarship test is held on the first Sunday of every month, and top scorers get a fee waiver.

## Refund policy
Fees are refundable within 7 days of admission if the student has attended no more than 3 classes.
