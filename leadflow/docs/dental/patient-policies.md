# Patient policies

## Appointments
Saturday mornings fill up first, so book a few days ahead. You can reschedule free of charge up to 4 hours before your slot.

## Emergencies
For severe pain, swelling or bleeding, call the clinic directly. We keep same-day slots for emergencies every day.

## Children
A parent or guardian must accompany patients under 18.

## Payments
We accept cards and UPI. No-cost EMI is available on treatments above ₹10,000.
