# Treatments at SmileCraft

## Braces and aligners
Treatment usually takes 12 to 24 months depending on the case. Children can start once most permanent teeth have come in, typically from about age 11. The first visit includes an examination, X-rays and a written treatment plan. Metal braces are at the lower end of the price range and clear aligners at the upper end.

## Root canal treatment
Usually completed in 2 visits. A crown is often recommended afterwards and is quoted separately after the dentist examines the tooth.

## Scaling and cleaning
Takes about 30 to 45 minutes. We recommend a cleaning every 6 months.

## Teeth whitening
A single in-clinic session of about 60 minutes. Results vary from person to person.
