# GST questions we get often

## Do I need GST registration?
Registration is mandatory once turnover crosses the threshold for your state and business type, and for most online sellers from day one. We check your case on the call.

## Documents for GST registration
PAN of the business or proprietor, Aadhaar, a photo, proof of business address such as a rent agreement with a recent electricity bill, and a cancelled cheque or bank statement.

## Monthly returns
We file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month and reconcile your purchases with GSTR-2B so you don't lose input tax credit. Send us your sales and purchase invoices by the 5th of every month.

## Invoice mismatches
If a supplier's invoice is missing or different in GSTR-2B, we contact the supplier with you and hold the credit until it matches.
