# ITR filing guide for individuals

## Documents we need
PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 from your employer, bank statements for the financial year, interest certificates, and proofs for deductions such as 80C investments and health insurance premiums.

## Freelance or side income
If you have freelance or consulting income along with salary, we file ITR-3 or ITR-4 depending on your case. Keep your invoices and the bank credits for the freelance work ready. We also check whether advance tax applied.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed by the Income Tax Department, usually within a few weeks of e-verification. We track the refund status for you after filing.
