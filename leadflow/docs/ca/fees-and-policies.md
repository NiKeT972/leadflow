# Fees and payment policy

## How we quote
Every quote is fixed before work starts. We confirm the final fee on the free 15-minute call once we have seen your documents.

## Payment
We accept UPI, bank transfer and cards. For one-time services, 50% is payable when you confirm and the rest on delivery. Monthly services such as GST returns and bookkeeping are billed at the start of each month.

## Turnaround
ITR filing is usually completed within 3 working days of receiving all documents. Private limited company registration usually takes 7 to 10 working days, depending on the Registrar of Companies. GST registration usually takes 5 to 7 working days after the application is submitted.

## Revised returns and notices
If you receive an income tax notice for a return we filed, we respond to it at no extra charge. Notices for returns filed by someone else are quoted separately.
